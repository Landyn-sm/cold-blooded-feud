# Sound credits

All files were downloaded on 2026-11-19, then trimmed (leading silence removed), faded,
loudness-normalised (about -16 LUFS for effects, -20 LUFS for themes) and re-encoded to
MP3 44.1 kHz stereo 192 kbps. None of the audio comes from a TV show.

Sounds were chosen from titles, descriptions, tags, download counts, ratings and
waveform/level analysis only. Nobody has listened to them yet, so audition each one
before publishing.

## Notes

- Mixkit files (buzzIn, fanfare, drumroll, theme and four alternates) may be used inside the
  game and the video, but the licence does not allow handing them out as standalone sounds.
  If this folder is ever published as a downloadable sound pack, swap them for the CC0 files.
- buzzer_alt2 is the uploader's edit of another Freesound doorbell-buzzer recording; the
  uploader released it as CC0 but the original was not traced.
- Three sources are known to be sample- or synth-based rather than live recordings:
  sting (GarageBand brass), fanfare_alt (MuseScore brass) and fmZero (a sampled real tuba note).
  Whether the Mixkit items are live recordings is not stated by Mixkit.
